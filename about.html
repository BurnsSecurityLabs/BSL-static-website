Hi, I’m Melissa 👋

I’m a security leader who thrives at the intersection of technology, trust, and teamwork. After years of partnering with engineering, product, and customer-facing teams to build security into product roadmaps, compliance workflows, and enterprise relationships, I am currently dedicated to upskilling, exploring new technology, and preparing for my next chapter.

Most recently, I served as a Senior Customer Trust Analyst at Abnormal AI, where I laid the foundation for the high-touch customer trust program. In this role, I designed the engagement framework for strategic enterprise accounts, partnered with Security, Engineering, Sales, and Legal to clear security hurdles during high-stakes deals, and migrated audit tracking workflows into Jira to streamline operations. Before Abnormal AI, I spent six years at Adobe leading cybersecurity and strategic remediation initiatives across multiple business units. My work focused on risk management, managing audit findings, and developing practical remediation strategies that balanced innovation with trust. Earlier in my career, I served as a Peace Corps (IT) volunteer in Vanuatu, helped U.S. government agencies build cybersecurity resilience, and kept systems running through hands-on IT maintenance and troubleshooting.

What drives me is real connection, whether that means translating complex compliance requirements into clear guidance, building customer trust programs, or helping leaders make informed risk decisions that keep organizations protected.

When I am not keeping up with security trends and new tech, you will usually find me outdoors running, doing yoga, or planning my next travel destination!
